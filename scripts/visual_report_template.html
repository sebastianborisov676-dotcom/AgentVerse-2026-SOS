    What these tests do not prove

    • The connector and inlet are simplified shapes, not the IEC 62196-3 drawing: no signal pins, latch or flap. "Seated" means 34 mm of pin engagement, not an electrical connection.
    • The cavities have clearance and no spring contacts, so a well-aligned insertion needs almost no force. A real CCS2 plug needs roughly 50–100 N.
    • Contact force is the physics solver's value. The controller acts on a 30 ms filtered signal (like a wrist force sensor); raw one-step peaks are shown in grey.
    • The controller is given the socket pose. "Socket estimate error" scenarios stand in for camera perception, which is not built yet.
    • Cable stiffness and weight are estimates, and the cable cannot collide with itself. The robot base does not drive.
